Assessment effects in action

Which way does the test push learning?

Washback (also called backwash) is the influence a test has on teaching and learning. Your task is to judge each classroom situation—and repair the harmful ones.

✓ Positive washback

The test encourages useful learning, authentic language use, sound teaching, and alignment with course goals.

! Negative washback

The test narrows, distorts, or harms learning—for example, by rewarding memorization while neglecting the intended skill.

Decision rule: Do not judge a test only by its format. Ask what teachers and learners are likely to do because of it—and whether that behavior supports meaningful learning.
